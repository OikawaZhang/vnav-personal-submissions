A. MESSAGE VS. TF

1. Convert an incoming geometry_msgs::Quaternion to an existing
   tf2::Quaternion:

     tf2::fromMsg(quat_msg, quat_tf);

2. Convert a tf2::Quaternion to a newly declared
   geometry_msgs::Quaternion:

     geometry_msgs::Quaternion quat_msg = tf2::toMsg(quat_tf);

3. Access the scalar component:

     double w = quat_tf.getW();

B. CONVERSION

1. Extract yaw from a tf2::Quaternion:

     double yaw = tf2::getYaw(quat_t);

2. Convert a geometry_msgs::Quaternion to an Eigen 3-by-3 rotation matrix:

     Eigen::Quaterniond quat_eigen;
     tf::quaternionMsgToEigen(quat_msg, quat_eigen);
     Eigen::Matrix3d R = quat_eigen.normalized().toRotationMatrix();

   Relevant headers include tf2_geometry_msgs/tf2_geometry_msgs.h,
   tf2/utils.h, eigen_conversions/eigen_msg.h, and Eigen/Geometry.
